# villa #1481 — flat ink `robust_mad` folds reader padding into stats

Upstream: https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/issues/1481  
Evidence: `villa-1481/`  
Prior unmerged attempts: #1483, #1646, #1773.  
Author on patch: Scroll Ash Lab \<scrollash.lab@gmail.com\> (AI-assisted)

## Problem

Flat ink **training** normalizes only voxels read from the source and leaves reader padding at zero. Flat **inference** padded first, then ran `normalize_robust()` over the whole tensor, so artificial zeros changed percentiles, median, and MAD for every real CT voxel — a train/infer mismatch.

Issue’s deterministic toy (3×4×4 source `100..147` centered into 5×4×4):

| | train semantics | buggy inference |
|--|----------------:|----------------:|
| padded zeros | `0` | `≈ -2.266` |
| real-voxel MAE | — | `≈ 0.488` |
| real-voxel max AE | — | `≈ 1.136` |

## What changed

Share placement between `FlatPatchReader.read` and normalization via `source_placement` / `valid_region_zyx`. For `tifxyz_robust`, normalize only that region; leave padding at zero. `divide_255` unchanged. Follows the placement-shared approach from the earlier unmerged PRs.
