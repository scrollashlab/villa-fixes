# villa #1648 — report depth order and in-plane scale

Upstream: https://github.com/ScrollPrize/villa/issues/1648  
Evidence: `villa-1648/`  
Author on patch: Scroll Ash Lab \<scrollash.lab@gmail.com\> (AI-assisted)

## Problem

Flat ink inference did not clearly report the effective depth orientation and in-plane scale users were running under. Orientation is segment/render-dependent; silently assuming one depth direction (or prescribing reverse) is unsafe. Fine in-plane scales relative to the ~9.6 µm training regime also need an explicit warning.

## What changed

In flat ink `infer.py`:

- **INFO** of effective `depth_order` and `in_plane_scale_um`
- **INFO** that orientation is segment/render-dependent; when undeclared, run `--direction both` (do **not** prescribe reverse; do **not** WARN on forward alone)
- **WARNING** only when in-plane scale is much finer than ~9.6 µm (`scale < 0.55 * 9.6`)
- Default `--direction` remains `forward`

### Pre-registered held-out matrix (3 segments, 2026-10-08)

The single-window result above was re-tested on all three `online_validation_cases` that ship a `validation_mask`: pherc0139-w016, pherc0814-46527 and pherc1667-w029.
- The kill test was pre-registered before any inference (`heldout-matrix-2026-10-08/preregistration.md`, sha256 `157a375a…2ec6`, sealed 2026-10-08 07:57 HST).
- Scoring used whole validation masks only. The L2 input (~9.6 µm) was compared with the L0 input (~2.4 µm) of the same crop: 2 seeds × 2 directions, villa main 8b63a2652, unpatched CLI, RTX 5060, torch 2.14.1+cu130.
- L0 output was mean-pooled 4×4 onto the L2 grid.
- Exact Mann–Whitney AUC; 95% CI from a paired 64×64-px block bootstrap (B=2000).

| segment | seed | AUC L2 fwd | AUC L0 fwd | Δ (L2−L0) | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pherc0139-w016 | 42 | 0.883 | 0.568 | +0.315 | [+0.257, +0.372] |
| pherc0139-w016 | 43 | 0.914 | 0.603 | +0.312 | [+0.245, +0.386] |
| pherc0814-46527 | 42 | 0.886 | 0.639 | +0.247 | [+0.177, +0.307] |
| pherc0814-46527 | 43 | 0.857 | 0.670 | +0.187 | [+0.114, +0.256] |

The operative direction was forward on all three segments, chosen by the mean of all 4 AUCs, a choice symmetric in L0/L2.

**Verdict: CONFIRM (3/3 segments; 2/3 required).** The in-plane-scale WARNING stays as written; the patch is unchanged.
- The result also holds when L0 is requantized to uint8.
- Reverse-direction results and per-run average precision are in `heldout-matrix-2026-10-08/` (`summary.md`, `metrics.json`).
- Re-verified: `git am` on main 8b63a2652, then pytest: **6 passed**.

**Caveats:**
- All three are training segments with validation-mask carve-outs, so spatial adjacency leakage within a segment cannot be excluded.
- 46527 overlaps the earlier exploratory window.
- The GPU masked-vs-unmasked check showed ±1/255 differences, so per the pre-registration all runs used the plain unmasked CLI (`deviations.md`).

### Depth-order flip vs the issue

On public S3 w035, CLI **`forward` beats `reverse`** for open-data L2 labels and HF aligned ch10 (train Z window or center pool). HF vs open agree **99.31%** on window A. Holdout 46527 also prefers forward at L2.

We **could not reproduce** the issue’s reverse-better w035 table on this store. Tutorial: `--flip-normals` “reproduces the depth orientation of the published renders and the training labels.” Likely the issue’s renderer/prepare path differs from raw public zarr. **Limitation:** author’s exact `prepare_9um_isotropic_input` CLI was not re-run end-to-end. Neutral wording (recommend `--direction both`) remains correct.

## Limitations

- 46527, w016 and w029 are training *segments*; each holdout is a spatial validation-mask carve-out (adjacency leakage possible per public ink_9um audits).
- w035 A/B must not be cited as generalization.
- Depth flip mismatch with the issue is unresolved without the author’s exact prepare path.
- Unit tests: **6 passed** after `git am` (`tests/test_inference_orientation_1648.py`).
